How FoodBridge Works

A direct, zero-cost bridge between food surplus and community hunger.

1

Mess Uploads Daily Extra Food

After lunch or dinner shifts, mess managers log in, specify available plates (e.g., 40 plates of Dal Rice), and set the freshness deadline.

2

NGOs Recommended Closest First

The platform calculates real-time distances using geolocation. Orphanages and NGOs near the mess receive top recommendations to ensure rapid collection.

3

Claim, Pick Up & Feed For Free

NGOs claim the listing with 1 click, view direct mess contact details and Google Maps directions, and collect the food before expiration.
